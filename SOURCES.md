# News sources for the 20 November 2026 front page

Every story on the page was reported on Friday 20 November 2026.
Headlines were condensed for legibility; no facts were added.

1. **Shenzhen summit** — Reuters, "Trump's personalized diplomacy
   hits its limits with 3-way summit" (20 Nov 2026): the first
   US-China-Russia three-way meeting, in Shenzhen during APEC;
   a symbolic peace declaration reaffirming that nuclear war should
   never be fought; no plans for arms-control talks and no advances
   on Iran, Ukraine, Taiwan, trade or AI; Trump told NBC the meeting
   was "a 12" on a scale of zero to 10.
2. **Kyiv barrage** — South China Morning Post world page (20 Nov
   2026): a daytime Russian barrage in Kyiv blew out windows at the
   Austrian and Dutch embassies and damaged a cathedral; the EU said
   it would summon Moscow's envoy. Appistos world wire (20 Nov):
   the UK pledged over 1,000 drones for Ukraine in a GBP 260 million
   package.
3. **Myanmar verdict** — Bangkok Post world page (20 Nov 2026):
   "Myanmar guilty of genocide against Rohingya, says top UN court"
   and "UN General Assembly chief tells Myanmar to 'bring Rohingya
   home'"; Malaysia said it would resettle 10,000 Rohingya refugees
   in Turkey.
4. **Riyadh attack** — South China Morning Post (20 Nov 2026):
   Riyadh's tallest skyscraper was hit in a Houthi attack, blowing
   out windows across eight floors, a second assault on the
   financial district this month. Reuters (20 Nov): oil prices
   edged higher as the attacks raised supply concerns.
5. **COP31** — Reuters (19-20 Nov 2026) and Dawn (20 Nov 2026):
   negotiators in Antalya published a revised draft deal with every
   mention of fossil fuels stripped out, as the talks reached their
   final day; the $120bn adaptation-finance target was also dropped,
   while Turkiye's "35 by 35" electrification goal stayed in.
6. **Voyager 1** — Associated Press via Manila Times (20 Nov 2026)
   and Reuters (19 Nov): NASA confirmed Voyager 1 had phoned home
   from one light-day away — about 16 billion miles (26 billion km);
   its signal took 24 hours to reach Earth, 49 years after launch.
7. **Anthropic IPO** — Reuters podcast brief (20 Nov 2026):
   Anthropic raised a record $81 billion in its IPO at a $1.5
   trillion valuation. Jiji (20 Nov): the shares closed up 17% on
   their first day of trading.
8. **Ticker** — Reuters (20 Nov 2026): a Seoul appeals court upheld
   the acquittal of Kakao founder Kim Beom-su on stock-manipulation
   charges; Taiwan flagged nearly 2,700 China-linked social-media
   accounts to Meta ahead of the 28 November local elections.
